### Fixed
- **Sessions keep each synth's MIDI inputs (#124):** a saved session now
  remembers which inputs every CLAP synth listens to: the keyboard switch,
  the hardware switch and the one MIDI device it is bound to. After a reload
  such a synth used to listen to the keyboard and to every hardware device
  again. The device is saved by its name, so it is found again after a
  restart or a re-plug; if it is not plugged in, the synth waits for it
  (shown as offline in the MIDI matrix) instead of playing from every other
  keyboard. Inserting or removing an effect in the mixer no longer resets
  these switches either. Older sessions load as before.
