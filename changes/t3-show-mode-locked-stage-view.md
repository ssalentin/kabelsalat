### Added
- **Show mode (#210):** a locked full-window stage screen with the current song and part in large type, the next song, tempo, the master meter, one meter per live rig, a clock and a Panic button. No click or key edits the patch, the mix or the setlist while it is open; PageUp / PageDown and foot switches still step parts, and holding Esc leaves. Agents open and leave it with `set_show_mode`.
- **Panic (#210):** all notes off on every rig, on every MIDI channel, with pedals up and arps and demo phrases stopped (`panic` for agents).
